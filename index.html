The Island

Welcome to Svefneyjar — a remote archipelago in Breiðafjörður, Iceland. It’s quiet here. Sea and sky, eider ducks and seals. The main lodge sleeps 12 across warm, minimal rooms. There’s a sauna, a firepit, a big table that becomes a war room. And bandwidth that keeps up with ambition.

  • 12 bedrooms, 14 guests
  • High‑speed Starlink internet
  • Sauna, firepit, ocean decks
  • Chef‑led or off‑grid options
  • Private boat access only
